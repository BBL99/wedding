Where to Stay

You are warmly invited to stay at the venue itself. La Mandriola has rooms in its 15th-century manor house, as well as self-contained apartments and villas.

Rooms are limited. To reserve a spot, please email us at clarice.giuseppe2027@gmail.com as early as possible and we will arrange it with the venue.

By Car & Parking

Hiring a car is the easiest way to explore the area. From Pisa airport the drive is around 50 minutes, mostly via the FI-PI-LI motorway and the SS439 towards Lajatico. Free parking is available on-site.

Sat-nav: Località La Mandriola, 56030 Lajatico (PI)

Airport Transfers

If you would rather not drive, the venue can arrange a private transfer from Pisa or Florence airport. Contact them directly to book.
