The Day

Saturday, 21 August 2027 — La Mandriola, Tuscany

2:00 PM

Guests Arrive

The venue team will show guests to their rooms. Once settled, everyone is welcome to gather and relax in the garden.

4:00 PM

Ceremony

The wedding ceremony takes place in the garden. Duration approximately 45 minutes.

4:45 PM

Drinks Reception

Prosecco and Tuscan appetizers served by the pool. Photographs will be taken during this time.

6:00 PM

Dinner & Speeches

Sit-down dinner on the terrace, followed by speeches from the wedding party.

8:00 PM

First Dance & Evening Reception

The dance floor opens after the first dance. Music and an open bar continue through the evening.

12:00 AM

Close of Evening

The venue is open until late. For those staying in other accommodation, we recommend booking a taxi in advance.

✦

Dress code: Smart, warm-weather attire. August in Tuscany is hot, with daytime temperatures often reaching the low-to-mid 30s°C. We suggest light, breathable fabrics, and a hat or sunglasses for the outdoor moments. The venue has lawn and gravel areas, so guests wearing heels may wish to bring flat shoes too.
