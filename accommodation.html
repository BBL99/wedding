Staying at the Venue

Accommodation at La Mandriola is limited, and we expect places to fill quickly. If you would like to book a room, please email us at clarice.giuseppe2027@gmail.com or mention it when you submit your RSVP. We will coordinate with the venue on your behalf.

Rooms are €80 per person per night, breakfast included.

Staying Nearby

If on-site rooms are full, there is plenty of lovely accommodation in the surrounding area. The nearby towns of Lajatico, Volterra, and San Miniato all have hotels, B&Bs, and agriturismo options within easy driving distance of the venue.
