The Day

Saturday, 21 August 2027 — La Mandriola, Tuscany

1:30 PM

Guests Arrive

Please take your seats in the ceremony barn. Drinks and canapés will be served on the lawn for early arrivals.

2:00 PM

Ceremony

The wedding ceremony takes place in the main barn. Duration approximately 45 minutes.

2:45 PM

Drinks Reception

Prosecco, elderflower cordial, and canapés on the garden terrace. Photographs will be taken during this time.

5:00 PM

Wedding Breakfast

A three-course sit-down meal in the dining hall, followed by speeches from the wedding party.

7:30 PM

First Dance & Evening Reception

The dance floor opens after the first dance. Evening guests are welcome from 7:30 PM. Live music and the bar continue until midnight.

12:00 AM

Close of Evening

The venue closes at midnight. We recommend booking a taxi in advance — see the Travel page for local numbers.

✦

Dress code: Smart, warm-weather attire. August in Tuscany is hot — daytime temperatures often reach the low-to-mid 30s°C — so we suggest light, breathable fabrics, and a hat or sunglasses for the outdoor moments. The venue has lawn and gravel areas, so guests wearing heels may wish to bring flat shoes too.
