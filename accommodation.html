Staying at La Mandriola

Everything you need to book a room at the venue for the wedding weekend.

✦

How to Book

Booking is done via bank transfer. When making your payment, please include in the reason for payment: the date of the wedding and the names of the people who will be staying overnight.

There is no need to choose a specific room or building — La Mandriola will arrange guests across their apartments and rooms.

Pricing

€80 per person — breakfast is included.
